# Similar apps and what we took from them

Date: 2026-10-20. Sources are company profiles, press and store listings (self-reported figures, not audited). We cannot
download or test these apps here, so this is desk research.

| App | What it does | Applied in DeliveryGram |
|---|---|---|
| Rozana (UP, Haryana) | Village "peer partners" onboard customers, help them order and deliver. Fixed delivery windows with order cut-offs (order by 9 pm for morning, by 4 pm for evening). Recurring daily orders. Wallet. | Agent role and assisted registration (done). **Not yet:** delivery windows with cut-offs, recurring orders, wallet. |
| 1K Kirana | Shopping app for neighbourhood stores; shops are franchise partners earning commission; customers can have orders delivered or pick them up. | **Pickup at the shop** (done): per-shop choice, no delivery fee for pickup bills, shop hands over. |
| VilCart | Supplies kirana shops in villages; shopkeepers lose days restocking from wholesalers. | **Not yet:** shop-side wholesale ordering (a later feature). |
| ApnaKlub | Agent-led wholesale ordering for rural shops. | Confirms the agent-led model. |
| OkCredit / Khatabook | Free digital khata. Customers install nothing: they get SMS records and payment reminders with a UPI pay option; 11 languages. | **Khata book for shopkeepers** with free tap-to-send **WhatsApp/SMS reminder links** (done). **Not yet:** UPI pay link in the reminder, auto-scheduled reminders, voice entry. |
| ONDC | Open network; any seller app and buyer app interoperate (Beckn protocol); free to join. | Possible later integration so our shops appear on other buyer apps. Not now. |
